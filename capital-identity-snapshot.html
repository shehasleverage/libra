About this exercise

This is the Week 1 exercise of the She Has Leverage x Libra Leaders LP Community Education Series. Your Capital Identity Statement becomes the first page of your Aligned LP Profile.

It's a short version of the Capital Identity Statement Builder from my Aligned Capital Program. The four money scripts come from research by Brad Klontz and colleagues. The descriptions on this page are my own summaries. The strength suggestions are reflection prompts created for this exercise.

Nothing here asks for amounts, account details or net worth, and nothing you type leaves this browser unless you copy or save it yourself.

Sources
