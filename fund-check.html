What this is, and isn’t

Five plain checks and the questions behind them. There is no score, because a fund is not a quiz; there is only what you have verified and what you haven’t. The “what to listen for” notes describe what strong and weak answers tend to sound like; they are not a judgment of any fund. The full diligence method, with the scorecard and the document walk-through, is part of the She Has Leverage program. Every venture term used here, and about eighty more, is in the glossary.
