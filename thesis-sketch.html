Session 2 exercise

Your Thesis Sketch

Three sentences you can say out loud without notes. What I believe. Where I look. What I say no to. Ten minutes, and they only need to be true, not polished.

Everything you type stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to She Has Leverage or Libra Leaders.

From your Week 1 Snapshot

Found on this device. Use it as a starting point for sentence 1, or ignore it.

Before the sentences, about 1 minute

The values you want in your thesis

Pick the ones that are yours, or write your own. These are not criteria and nobody scores them; they are the words you should be able to find inside your three sentences when you read them back.

Sentence 1 of 3, about 3 minutes

What I believe

A change in the world you are confident about and most investors still underrate. The strongest version comes from something you lived, at work or at home, for years.

An example, deliberately not yoursI believe employers will pay for women’s health because it keeps their people at work.
The health decisions you made for your familyThe people you hired and managedThe compliance rules you worked underThe risk you were accountable forSomething else you lived for years

Sentence 2 of 3, about 3 minutes

Where I look

The stage, the kind of company and the kind of manager that fit the belief above. “Early stage” and “a manager who has sold into that buyer” are both answers; so is “I don’t know the stage yet.”

An example, deliberately not yoursSo I look at early-stage funds run by people who have sold into that buyer themselves.
Which stage: the idea, the first customers, or growth?What kind of person do you trust to pick the companies?

Sentence 3 of 3, about 3 minutes

What I say no to

The investment that would be fine for someone else and is not for you. At least one thing, said plainly. A thesis without a “no” is a wish list, and a fund deck will never ask you this.

An example, deliberately not yoursAnd I pass on anything I couldn’t explain to my daughter.
What would you not want your name next to, even if it made money?What have you already turned down, and why?
Read it together, 1 minute

Does this sound like you?

If a sentence sounds like something you read rather than something you think, go back and change it. Can you find your values inside the three sentences? If one is missing, it either belongs in a sentence or it isn’t a value you invest on. The one you will be asked to share out loud is any of the three.
